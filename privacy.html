Privacy Policy for Tab Setu

Effective date: January 2026

Tab Setu is a browser extension designed to help users manage and organize their browser tabs.

Information We Access

Tab Setu may access browser tab information such as tab titles, URLs, and window details only to provide its tab management features.

Information We Collect

Tab Setu does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to any external server.

Local Storage

Any data saved by Tab Setu is stored locally in the user's browser using browser storage.

Third-Party Sharing

Tab Setu does not share user data with third parties.

Analytics and Advertising

Tab Setu does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data Deletion

Users can delete stored data by clearing the extension's storage, resetting the extension, or uninstalling Tab Setu.
